How to use this
  • One ChatGPT chat per section (A, B, E …). Paste the STYLE line first, then the prompts one at a time.

  • Make the picture again if it breaks the rules: perspective in a flat view, writing you didn't ask for, a busy background, wrong proportions or a missing view.
  • Keep the little ID label in the bottom-left corner (A01, B03 …). That's how Claude sorts your zip.

The layouts the prompts name
  • SHEET: one object alone on a plain light grey background, in soft even light with no strong shadows. True orthographic views (no perspective), all at the same scale: front, side, back and top, plus one three-quarter view. A 1 m scale bar. Small view names under each view. Landscape 3:2.
  • SET: several separate objects on one plain light grey sheet, each shown front, side and top at a shared scale, spaced apart, with a scale bar (1 m, or 10 cm for small items). Landscape 3:2.
  • SCENE: a single view of a place or an interior, as a reference for building it, at eye level or from the air as the prompt says. Even daylight. Landscape 3:2.
  • TEXTURE: seamless and tileable. Seen straight down (or straight on, for walls), in flat even light with no shadows or highlights, no objects and no perspective. Square 1:1. Each prompt says how much ground it covers.
  • PANORAMA: a very wide painted backdrop (as wide as ChatGPT allows). Only the distant land. Its bottom edge is a soft haze line where it meets the ground, and the sky fades to plain near the top. No foreground and no sun disc.
  • PORTRAIT: head and shoulders of a weathered real person in work clothes, outdoor light, a plain softly blurred background. Square. For a radio contact card.
  • ANIMAL: one animal on plain light grey: side view standing, front view, and side view running, all at the same scale, with a scale bar. Landscape 3:2.
  • PAINT: a new paint job on the template picture attached. Keep every view exactly where it is and the same size, and change only the paint and its wear.
